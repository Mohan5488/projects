Electronic Health Record Management System (EHRMS)

Abstract

Previous healthcare systems faced challenges like fragmented health records, leading to delays in accessing complete medical information, especially during emergencies. Issues like difficulty in data sharing, reliance on manual appointment booking, and partial digital records led to inefficiencies, including repeated tests and increased waiting times. Security concerns around digital records further compounded these problems.

This project aims to centralize health records into a secure digital platform, providing easy access to personal medical data and simplifying appointment booking with healthcare providers. By integrating records with Aadhaar, the system allows for secure storage and retrieval of health data. This approach aims to reduce waiting times, minimize unnecessary medical tests, and improve emergency care by ensuring that doctors have immediate access to complete patient information. This system offers a streamlined, user-friendly experience for patients and improves overall healthcare delivery.
